OFFLINE BUILDER VERSION: 24

LAND OF EEM CHARACTER BUILDER — OFFLINE EDITION

1. Download this ZIP and extract/unzip it completely.
2. Open the Land-of-Eem-Offline folder.
3. Double-click index.html to open the builder in your browser.
   If prompted, choose Microsoft Edge or Google Chrome.

No installation, account, local server, or internet connection is required.
Character creation, editable story examples, Roll20 copy buttons, PDF preview,
printing, and PDF download are included.

KEEP YOUR CHARACTER
Use Save file to download an .eem.json character file. Use Load file to open
it again. Browser autosave may depend on your browser and the location of
index.html, so keep a character file as your backup before moving the builder.
Each friend uses their own character files. Drafts are not shared or synced.
Character files from the online builder work here, and vice versa.

OWLBEAR RODEO
Open Sheet & Roll20 and click Export Owlbear JSON. In Blaze Sanecki’s Land of
Eem extension, choose a save slot, click Import, and open the .owlbear.json file.
Import replaces that slot. Keep your .eem.json Save file for editing here.
Conditional equipment effects and fixed Dread bonuses need manual application
in the extension. Current Courage is exported at maximum.

PRINTING AND PDF
Open Preview sheet, then choose Print or Download PDF.
If your browser blocks a download, choose Open PDF and save from its viewer.
The original sheet artwork is included. Long entries use continuation pages.
PDF preparation can take a moment; wait for the download button to enable.

SHARING
Send this ZIP to your friends, including through OneDrive. Each person downloads
and extracts their own copy. Open the extracted index.html, not a OneDrive
preview or the file while it is still inside the ZIP.
